# Background Fill Toy

## What it is

A toy that fills empty backgrounds with animated 3D steps. A board of blocks in an X ridge — two diagonal rows crossing at a center peak, stepping down on each side — that you can recolor, relight, tilt, and put your own image on. I needed a fresh background for an animated ANSI/ASCII art project. This was the outcome. 

## How to use

1. Open the page. The board is already there.
2. Pick a field size and how many steps down.
3. Shuffle the pattern, or Reset back to the clean X.
4. Pick a fill color, a wrapper frame color, and a background.
5. Pick the lighting and the speed.
6. Tilt the board, or drag it to tilt.
7. Upload any image, size it, and place it.
8. Record it with your own screen capture.

## Lighting

There are four lighting modes. What each one does is different.

- **Random twinkle** — blocks light up at random, in the Koishii colors.
- **Center out** — single-block rays fire from the center outward, several at once, in random directions.
- **Both** — twinkle and center-out rays together.
- **Off** — the animation stops completely.

A ray never lights more than one block at a time.

## Colors

- **Fill** — one solid color on all the blocks. Grey or a color you pick.
- **Wrapper frame** — a colored frame around the board, with its own color picker.
- **Background** — presets, or a color you pick.

## Images

Upload any image — lettering, a logo, anything. Size it, set the opacity, drag it where you want it, or lay it flat on the board or facing you. Nothing is saved — upload your own each time you open the page.

## Phones

The toy runs on phones. There is no download button — record it with your own screen capture.

## Common questions

**Where is the download button?**
There isn't one. Use your own screen capture to record it.

**Can I save my image in the page?**
No. Nothing is saved. Upload your own each time.

**Can I use any color with any lighting?**
Yes. Pick any combination.
